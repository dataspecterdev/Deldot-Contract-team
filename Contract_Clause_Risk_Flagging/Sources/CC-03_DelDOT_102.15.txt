102.15 Non-Collusive Bidding Certification.

A. Provide the signed non-collusive bidding certification form provided in the bid proposal.

B. The Department will not consider a proposal for award without the signed non-collusive bidding
certification.

C. The Department does not consider bid collusion to include the following:
1. A bidder has published price lists, rates, or tariffs covering items in the proposal.
2. A bidder has informed prospective customers of proposed or pending publication of new or
revised price lists for proposal items.
3. A bidder sold the same items to other customers at the same prices in the proposal.

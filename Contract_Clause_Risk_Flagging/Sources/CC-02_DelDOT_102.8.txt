102.8 Proposal Guaranty.

A. Provide either a bid bond from a corporate surety authorized to do business in Delaware or some
form of financial security the bidder assigns to the Department. Bid securities include certified
checks, cashier's checks, treasurer's checks, or other negotiable or transferable instruments
evidencing an unconditional debt to the State or Department. Use the Department provided bond
form. Provide a proposal guaranty equal to 10 percent of the total bid price. The bid bond does
not need to cite a specific sum of money but does need to state that the bond equals 10 percent
of the total bid price.

Reference: DelDOT proposal General Notices
Source: Right to Audit
Requirement ID: CC-13

Note: The "Right to Audit" clause appears in the General Notices section of DelDOT proposals. The system should check the contract package's Proposal and General Notices document for this section.

Challenge Reference Rule: For challenge scoring, relevant prime-contractor and subcontractor records supporting contract performance must be available for audit and retained for three years after final payment.

Review Expectation: Verify audit rights are preserved and the stated records-retention period is not shortened.

Key Requirements to Check:
- Audit rights cover both prime contractor and subcontractor records
- Records relate to contract performance
- Retention period is three years after final payment
- Records must be available for audit (not restricted or excluded)
